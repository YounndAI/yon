### Changed

Initial release. Convenience meta-package for the YON v2.0 toolchain — depends on and re-exports the eight tool packages (`@younndai/{yon-parser,yon-generator,yon-runner,yon-converter,domains,ai-relay,yon-examples,yon-benchmarks}`). Version aligns with the toolchain line.
